\title{Code to compute $\ell_{P}$ and detect K-unstability}

\maketitle
Let $P\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a lattice polytope which is defined
by 
\[
P=\left\{ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\mid\left\langle x,v_{i}\right\rangle +a_{i}\ge0,\ \forall1\le i\le d\right\} ,
\]
where $v_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}^{n}$ are primitive vectors and $a_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}$.
Let $\mathrm{d}\sigma$ be the lattice measure on the boundary $\partial P$,
which is defined as follows. Consider a facet of $P$ 
\[
F_{i}=\left\{ x\in P\mid\left\langle x,v_{i}\right\rangle +a_{i}=0\right\} .
\]
Define 
\[
\mathrm{d}\sigma|_{F_{i}}=\frac{\mathrm{d}s}{\left|v_{i}\right|}
\]
where $\mathrm{d}s$ is the Euclidean surface measure on $F_{i}$,
and $\left|v_{i}\right|$ is the Euclidean $2$-norm. Another way
to define $\mathrm{d}\sigma$: it is the Lebesgue measure on the affine
space spanned by $F_{i}$ which is normalized by the rank $n-1$ lattice
$F_{i}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{n}$. 

The relative DF invariant is 
\[
M_{\ell}(f)\coloneqq\int_{\partial P}f\mathrm{d}\sigma-\int_{P}f\ell_{P}\mathrm{d}x,\ \text{for convex }f:P\rightarrow\mathbb{R},
\]
where $\ell_{P}$ is the unique affine function such that 
\[
M_{\ell}(h)=\int_{\partial P}h\mathrm{d}\sigma-\int_{P}h\ell_{P}\mathrm{d}x=0,\ \text{for all affine function }h.
\]
Equivalently, take $h=1,x_{i}$, $\ell_{P}$ satisfies linear system:
\[
\int_{P}\ell_{P}=\left|\partial P\right|_{\mathrm{d}\sigma},\int_{P}x_{i}\ell_{P}=\int_{\partial P}x_{i}\mathrm{d}\sigma,\ i=1,\dots,n.
\]

\begin{defn}
We say $P$ is relatively K-semistable if $M_{\ell}(f)\geq0$ for
all $f\in\mathcal{C}(P)$, where $\mathcal{C}(P)$ is the space of
continuous functions on $P$. Otherwise, if $M_{\ell}(f)<0$ for some
$f\in\mathcal{C}(P)$, we say $P$ is relatively K-unstable. 
\end{defn}

\begin{fact}[Donaldson]
 when $n=2$, $P$ is relatively K-unstable if and only if there
is a simple convex function of the form 
\[
g=\max\left\{ ax+by+c,0\right\} 
\]
such that $M_{\ell}(g)<0$.
\end{fact}

Goal: 

1. compute $\ell_{P}$ precisely. 

2. develop an algorithm to test the un-stability of polygons using
the fact of Donaldson. 

3. 前面是给定多面体，探测它是否unstable. 现在我想搜索2维的relative K-unstable 多面体. 

输入面facet的个数$d$, 正整数$N$, 遍历$d$个primitive integer vector $v_{i}=\left(a_{i},b_{i}\right)\in\mathbb{Z}^{2}$
使得
\[
\gcd\left(a_{i},b_{i}\right)=1\text{ and }\max\left\{ \left|a_{i}\right|,\left|b_{i}\right|\right\} \le N.
\]
然后再选取 $t_{1},\dots,t_{d}$ 定义多面体
\[
P=\left\{ x\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\mid\left\langle x,v_{i}\right\rangle \ge t_{i},\ \forall1\le i\le d\right\} .
\]
要使得$P$没有hidden facet, 即每个$v_{i}$都对应着一个facet, 否则某些$v_{i}$是多余的. 然后check
$P$是否是relatively K-unstable? 可用现成的Donaldson简单凸函数判别程序. 

注意上述参数$v_{i},t_{i}$有自由度的冗余. 

(1) 对$\forall a>0$, $(t_{i})$ 和 $(at_{i})$ 是等价的, 因为$(at_{i})$对应$aP$,
与$P$的稳定性一致.

(2) 对$\forall y\in\mathbb{R}^{n}$, $(t_{i}+\left\langle y,v_{i}\right\rangle )$
和 $(t_{i})$ 是等价的. 因为前者对应着$P+y$, 与$P$的稳定性一致.
